           Introduction 
           ------------ 

Poems over a witch, Phaedrah. The work 
targets a singular goal: Protection. 
The protection is built by me such that 
I can depend on my feelings for her with 
little to no backfire from her end. The 
support for the protection is my feelings, 
Love. The need for the protection comes 
from the Trials. The promises for the 
protection are in Loyalty. 

           * * * 

My first practice 
with Phaedrah was to 
be possessed: 
Poet, Muse: 
Phaedrah, Morris. 

           Protection 
           ---------- 

           Flow 

her name needs redirection 
for her protection 
in this connection, 
I made for my satisfaction. 

           Distribute 

when I center into 
 my emotions, 
heavy for her. 
moods that finalize 
 my life, 
seducing me to live 
 slow. 

loveties won't let go, 
stronger than I've ever 
 known. 
scatter these feelings 
 into the water, 
the invisible energy 
 lulling us all. 

           Relief 

weave my feelings into 
 a cord. 
tether me to her. 
shake the excess weight 
into the atmosphere to be 
 absorbed. 

           Classroom 

straight hair 
of purple and silver. 
opal in her nose. 
energy in her pose. 
let her breathe more. 

the hospital's best 
 mover. 
everyone wants to be 
 closer. 
I wear my heart like 
 a rose. 
I give gifts to evoke. 
let the people move 
 over. 

           Dayroom 

as she blooms 
the room, 
dancing the full 
feelings to all, 
protect her from 
 doom. 

           Library 

in the library, 
each book is a wall 
for her sanctuary. 
the topics overall 
don't plant seeds 
in her mind to become 
 weeds. 

           Recovery 

in group, 
she is queen, 
keeping everyone 
 up. 
all discussed 
 and foreseen. 
routine secures 
 her pay. 
admiration 
 secures her 
 ways. 

           Love 
           ---- 

           Blackmen 

she owns the room patiently, 
a purple and silver rockstar. 
her voice is pitched to strum 
 my nerves, 
a siren guarded by everyone. 

           Trouble 

she finds me outside, 
black as shade in the 
 sun. 
telling me how to 
 deliver my gifts, 
lifting my hopes high 
 enough to carry my 
 loveaches. 

           Bradford 

the first time she 
 told my eyes 
 what she likes, 
my heart spiked: 
lovestruck for the 
 rest of my life. 

           Shy 

hot silver in her 
 hair. 
this gypsy is here 
 and there: 
rich in moons; 
a dark woman 
 wonders my moods; 
my eyes gleam away 
 from a stare. 

           Meditate 

memories of her 
are the most reliable 
 dose 
of happiness 
that made my attention 
 arose. 

           Sore 

how could I leave these feelings 
 behind? 
when they're so sore for her. 
when I can just keep her in mind. 
when I can give her what the space 
 is worth. 

           Bewitched 

this gypsy only wants to will 
 one thing: 
a thing for being 
witchbound because it sings. 

           Ecclesiastes 

hands to my chest, 
to my belly. 
incoming rest 
to satisfy. 

           Lucia 

she's made of love and crystal cells. 
glow springs from her into the room. 
her opal lips are jewels of dawn. 

           Seed 

she makes me feel 
small, safe, soft, 
escaped, and lost. 

           Trials 
           ------ 

           Outside 

madspells on the road. 
lovespells where we hold. 
to be her greatest effect. 
to be her cherished. 
to shed old molds. 

           Mastery 

voices and a host 
are touch and go. 
sight, taste, and smell 
are dispensable. 
twenty-four/seven composed. 

           Blues 

dear purple and silver 
I'm your walking blues, 
here and there. 
stay with me and stay true. 

           Detox 

hunger for more 
of what the street pours. 
be hers evermore 
as the rest nevermores. 

           Friends 

wind down the river of the street, 
the ghost of harmony sows streets. 
walk a large star to reap rewards. 

           Southward 

tremble in the gold heat midday, 
sweat full of curls and wind-filled 
 eyes. 
a cool spring's bank by the bottle.

           Poet 

love, book and dice in my knapsack, 
twisting them like labyrinths inside. 
asleep with echoes of writing. 

           Diffuse 

by the dead highway of winter, 
the landscape with arms spread wide, 
the silence becomes the snow sphinx. 

           Murals 

cell 114 in lenox, 
decorated with mental 
 art. 
cradled by her, 
unsure of the spirit 
 at hand. 

           Snake 

I chase other behaviors 
as I close in on Phaedrah. 

           Provider 

the fool in me 
won't focus on her 
just to see: 
I don't stop 
 delivering to her. 

           Worker 

the man in me 
can't find work 
better than 
what these feelings 
 are worth. 

           Roundrobin 

the voice shuffles 
to other speakers, 
like a bird ruffles 
his feathers. 

           Drinker 

what lies dormant at a tear's core? 
salt hydrates, water bears fruit. 
wind shapes a blade across my cheek. 

           Radioactive 

blues are reflected in the wind. 
my hot heart, a crowd of breezes. 
drooping trees in my mind sing songs. 

           Statue 

the mood in my walk, a dark breeze. 
my boots walk like a riverbed. 
lighting another cigarette. 

           Loyalty 
           ------- 

           Mine 

a mother 
with plenty of time 
to be bothered; 
and I climb 
to befriend, 
if no other end. 

           Curse 

cursed to walk the blues. 
cursed to love true. 
cursed to stay aware 
of a pair. 

           Glow 

of her only, 
do I think highly 
and feel wildly. 

           Savage 

there are seasons 
 for such and so. 
there is breathing 
 for all to go. 
put her in my breath, 
unto death. 
feelings for her are 
 how I stroll. 

           Bullseye 

keep my attention inward. 
that joyful feeling is forward. 

           Chills 

skin walker's chance: 
let the sensations dance. 

           Fin 

nothing more to solve. 
be with her above all. 

           Shock 

I feel her presence closely, 
   inwardly possessed. 
I want no other nearby. 

           Focused 

hand in hand, we're together 
   without doubt. 
no item keeping me busy. 

           Dawn 

I wake up to the bluing 
 skies, 
lower temperatures raise 
 the sun up. 
I grab her in my hand, 
like a baby. 

           Mule 

she resides in my 
 haptics 
and acoustics, 
to stay closest. 
I bear the weight of my 
 interests. 

           Blame 

I take all the 
 blame for this affair. 
although blame is 
 not taken up by my 
 affairs: 
this happens, 
she is there.
