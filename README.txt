           Introduction 
           ------------ 

Spells and poems over a witch, 
Phaedrah. The work targets a singular 
goal: Protection. The protection is built 
by me such that I can depend on my 
feelings for her with little to no 
backfire from her end. The support for 
the protection is my feelings, Love. The 
need for the protection comes from the 
Trials. The promises for the protection 
are in Loyalty. 

These poems are directed at her, but 
they are for me: for my end of this 
connection. 

           Preface 
           ------- 

I. 
My first practice 
with Phaedrah was to 
be possessed: 
Poet, Muse: 
Phaedrah, Morris. 

           Protection 
           ---------- 

II. 
her name needs redirection 
for her protection 
in this connection, 
I made for my satisfaction. 

III. 
when I center into 
 my emotions, 
heavy for her. 
moods that finalize 
 my life, 
seducing me to live 
 slow. 

loveties won't let go, 
stronger than I've ever 
 known. 
scatter these feelings 
 into the water, 
the invisible energy 
 lulling us all. 

IV. 
weave my feelings into 
 a cord. 
tether me to her. 
shake the excess weight 
into the atmosphere to be 
 absorbed. 

V. 
straight hair 
of purple and silver. 
opal in her nose. 
energy in her pose. 
let her breathe more. 

the hospital's best 
 mover. 
everyone wants to be 
 closer. 
I wear my heart like 
 a rose. 
I give gifts to evoke. 
let the people move 
 over. 

VI. 
as she blooms 
the room, 
dancing the full 
feelings to all, 
protect her from 
 doom. 

VII. 
in the library, 
each book is a wall 
for her sanctuary. 
the topics overall 
don't plant seeds 
in her mind to become 
 weeds. 

VIII. 
in group, 
she is queen, 
keeping everyone 
 up. 
all discussed 
 and foreseen. 
routine secures 
 her pay. 
admiration 
 secures her 
 ways. 

IX. 
she resides in my 
 haptics 
and acoustics, 
to stay closest. 
I bear the weight of my 
 interests. 

X. 
I take all the 
 blame for this affair. 
although blame is 
 not taken up by my 
 affairs: 
this happens, 
she is there. 

           Love 
           ---- 

XI. 
she owns the room patiently, 
a purple and silver rockstar. 
her voice is pitched to strum 
 my nerves, 
a siren guarded by everyone. 

XII. 
she finds me outside, 
black as shade in the 
 sun. 
telling me how to 
 deliver my gifts, 
lifting my hopes high 
 enough to carry my 
 loveaches. 

XIII. 
the first time she 
 told my eyes 
 what she likes, 
my heart spiked: 
lovestruck for the 
 rest of my life. 

XIV. 
hot silver in her 
 hair. 
this gypsy is here 
 and there: 
rich in moons; 
a dark woman 
 wonders my moods; 
my eyes gleam away 
 from a stare. 

XV. 
memories of her 
are the most reliable 
 dose 
of happiness 
that made my attention 
 arose. 

XVI. 
how could I leave these feelings 
 behind? 
when they're so sore for her. 
when I can just keep her in mind. 
when I can give her what the space 
 is worth. 

XVII. 
this gypsy only wants to will 
 one thing: 
a thing for being 
witchbound because it sings. 

XVIII. 
hands to my chest, 
to my belly. 
incoming rest 
to satisfy. 

           Trials 
           ------ 

XIX. 
cell 114 in lenox, 
decorated with mental 
 art. 
cradled by her, 
unsure of the spirit 
 at hand. 

XX. 
I chase other behaviors 
as I close in on Phaedrah. 

XXI. 
madspells on the road. 
lovespells where we hold. 
to be her greatest effect. 
to be her cherished. 
to shed old molds. 

XXII. 
voices and a host 
are touch and go. 
sight, taste, and smell 
are dispensable. 
twenty-four/seven composed. 

XXIII. 
dear purple and silver 
I'm your walking blues, 
here and there. 
stay with me and stay true. 

XXIV. 
hunger for more 
of what the street pours. 
be hers evermore 
as the rest nevermores. 

XXV. 
the fool in me 
doesn't focus on her 
just to see: 
I don't stop 
 delivering to her. 

XXVI. 
the man in me 
can't find work 
better than 
what these feelings 
 are worth. 

XXVII. 
the voice shuffles 
to other speakers, 
like a bird ruffles 
his feathers. 

           Loyalty 
           ------- 

XXVIII. 
a mother 
with plenty of time 
to be bothered; 
and I climb 
to befriend, 
if no other end. 

XXIX. 
cursed to walk the blues. 
cursed to love true. 
cursed to stay aware 
of a pair. 

XXX. 
of her only, 
do I think highly 
and feel wildly. 

XXXI. 
I feel her presence closely, 
   inwardly possessed. 
I want no other nearby. 

XXXII. 
hand in hand, we're together 
   without doubt. 
no item keeping me busy. 

XXXIII. 
I wake up to the bluing 
 skies, 
lower temperatures raise 
 the sun up. 
I grab her in my hand, 
like a baby. 

XXXIV. 
there are seasons 
 for such and so. 
there is breathing 
 for all to go. 
put her in my breath, 
unto death. 
feelings for her are 
 how I stroll.
